The first five minutes

A judge arrives alone at 3 a.m. It still works.

The whole loop is five beats, walked in order — which is why they're numbered. Every beat works at concurrency one, and every beat leaves something behind for the next stranger.

  1. 1

    Arrive

    A night clearing: one fire, eight interest shrines, a ring of lanterns left by real past visitors. No sign-up, no form, no wallet ceremony.

  2. 2

    Declare

    Three taps on the shrines for what you love. The sparks settle as glowing charms on your avatar — everyone present can see what you're about before a word is exchanged.

  3. 3

    Answer

    One tap-to-answer icebreaker, keyed to your sparks. Your ember arcs into the central fire and the fire visibly grows — it is the sum of every answer ever given here.

  4. 4

    Meet

    The World names a real person who answered the same way — "You and Amara both said 'Quiet, please.'" — and a trail of light grows across the ground to their lantern. Live if they're here; across time if they're not.

  5. 5

    Leave a light

    You light a lantern addressed to the next stranger who shares your rarest spark. It will be there tomorrow. Someone will find it, the way you found one.

Nobody hosts it

The World remembers, so no one has to run it.

Most social venues die the moment the event ends. Common Ground doesn't hold events. The server owns every ember, lantern and match — state survives everyone leaving, and the plaza a visitor walks into is the accumulation of every visitor before them.

There is deliberately no chat system, no runtime AI, and no external backend. The conversation layer is authored content and shared taps — which means nothing to moderate, nothing to pay for, and nothing that breaks at 3 a.m.

  • sceneDecentraland SDK7 · TypeScript
  • stateDecentraland-hosted multiplayer server, authoritative + persistent
  • pairingdeterministic spark-overlap scoring — rarest shared spark wins
  • inputtaps only — no keyboard, no voice, no hover
  • backendnone beyond the platform · zero running cost
